BUILD PHASE 1 — DIGS JOBTRACK
================================

IMPORTANT:
This is PHASE 1 ONLY.

Do NOT start Phase 2 or Phase 3.
Do NOT add GPS navigation, live tracking, route optimization, satellite maps, geofencing, or advanced AI yet.

Do NOT throw away or unnecessarily rebuild existing work.
First inspect the existing project and preserve anything useful.
Then implement and improve Phase 1 below.

PROJECT NAME:
DIGS JobTrack

PURPOSE:
A professional mobile-first field-installation job management system for managing MTN ODU installation jobs received from WRKMAN.

The application should help me receive jobs, organize them, contact customers, track responses, schedule installations, record completed/failed/postponed jobs, keep evidence and expenses, and produce professional reports.

The application must be simple enough to operate quickly from an Android phone while working in the field.

7. CALL BUTTON
================================

Every customer must have:

CALL CUSTOMER

When clicked, open the Android/device phone dialer using the customer's phone number.

Do not require copying the number manually.

Record that the call was initiated.

After returning to the application, allow recording:

ANSWERED
NO ANSWER
UNREACHABLE
CALL FAILED
CUSTOMER REQUESTED CALLBACK

Create contact history.

Example:

21 Aug — Call — Answered
21 Aug — Call — No Answer
22 Aug — SMS — Sent

Do not falsely claim that the application can always determine whether a human answered a cellular call.

================================
8. SMS BUTTON
================================

Every customer must have:

SMS CUSTOMER

When clicked, open the phone's native SMS application with:

TO:
Customer phone number

and optionally pre-filled message text.

The user must still be able to review and press SEND.

Create editable SMS templates.

Template 1:

"Good day, sir/ma. My name is Israel. I'm contacting you from WRKMAN. I am assigned to install your MTN ODU at your location. Please kindly let me know when you will be available for the installation. You can also reach me on WhatsApp: [MY WHATSAPP NUMBER]. Thank you."

The user's own WhatsApp/phone number must be configurable from Settings.

Other templates:

CUSTOMER DID NOT ANSWER

"Good day, sir/ma. I tried reaching you concerning your MTN ODU installation assigned to me from WRKMAN. Kindly let me know when you will be available. You can also reach me on WhatsApp: [MY WHATSAPP NUMBER]. Thank you."

INSTALLATION CONFIRMATION

"Good day, sir/ma. This is Israel from WRKMAN regarding your MTN ODU installation. Kindly confirm your availability for the scheduled installation."

ON THE WAY

"Good day, sir/ma. This is Israel from WRKMAN. I am currently on my way for your MTN ODU installation."

FOLLOW-UP

"Good day, sir/ma. I'm following up regarding your MTN ODU installation. Kindly let me know when you will be available."

Allow templates to be edited from Settings.
